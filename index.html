Lingwa Explorers

Tap, listen and learn your first words in Maltese and English, in a colourful world made for little hands.

How it works

A little game for little learners

Every picture says its own name

Children explore colourful scenes and tap whatever catches their eye. Each object says its name in a real human voice and shows how the word is written, so a child hears the word and sees it at the same time.

A flag button switches between Maltese and English at any moment, so the same picture teaches both languages. It's made for children learning Maltese, children learning English, and children growing up with both.

Explore the scenes

No rules, no timer, no score. Wander the beach, dive under the sea or stroll through the park, and tap anything, as often as you like.

Treasure hunts

Find what the game asks for, by picture, by sound, or by the written word for children beginning to read.

Build your own

Drag things from the tray to make a scene of your own, tap each one to hear its name, and save up to five favourites.
